The Fragmented Presence Problem

Your online presence shouldn’t feel scattered.

Instagram, brokerage profiles, listing platforms, and property-search tools each serve a useful purpose. But prospective clients should not have to visit several places to understand who you are, what you specialize in, where you work, and how to reach you.

A personal website brings your brand, services, credibility, and contact pathways together in one professional place. It complements the platforms you already use—and gives them one clear destination to point back to.

How It Works

From consultation to launch, without the guesswork.

Typical project timeline Most projects are completed in approximately 2–3 weeks after onboarding is complete.

Timing depends on project complexity and timely client content and feedback.

  1. 01

    Consultation

    We start with a 30-minute conversation about your business, personal brand, current online presence, and what you want your website to accomplish. It is a no-obligation conversation to understand fit and needs.

  2. 02

    Agreement + Initial Payment

    If the project is a good fit, we confirm the scope in writing. Once the agreement is signed and the $225 initial payment is received, your founding-client project is reserved.

  3. 03

    Onboarding

    You’ll provide the business information, branding, photos, social links, content, and other materials needed for the website. Doorway Digital may organize and refine supplied content for clarity and presentation.

  4. 04

    Design + Development

    Doorway Digital turns the approved direction and supplied materials into your custom Realtor website, with the experience designed intentionally across desktop and mobile.

  5. 05

    Review + Revisions

    You review the completed website and provide consolidated feedback through the two revision rounds included with the Founding Client Program.

  6. 06

    Final Payment + Launch

    Once the agreed website is completed and approved, the remaining balance is paid before final launch, transfer, or handoff. Doorway Digital connects the agreed domain and setup and provides 30 days of post-launch technical support for issues related to the website as delivered. After full payment, ownership of the agreed final website deliverables transfers subject to applicable third-party licenses and terms.
