How the work happens

Simple. Clear. Collaborative.

After you pay the deposit, you should already know what happens next.

  1. 01

    Consultation

    A short conversation about you, your current presence, and what the website needs to do. Both of us decide whether it is a fit.

  2. 02

    Brand direction

    We establish structure, visual direction, and content needs. The project begins with the $750 deposit and the materials you send.

  3. 03

    Design & development

  4. 04

    Revisions

  5. 05

    Approval & launch

    Final approval, the remaining $750, domain connection, and launch. Ownership of the agreed deliverables transfers on full payment, subject to third-party terms.
